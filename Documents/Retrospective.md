# Retrospective

### What was Done Well?
- Large Amounts of Trash was cleaned up

- A Large-scale medical evaluation was held with relatively no issues

- We've reduced overall Pollution by 15% within the community

- We've reduced overall Sickness Rate by 12%

### What needs to be Done Better or Differently?
- The overall goal was somewhat ambiguous and caused issues, so if this project were to be done again there would be a downsizing of expectations.

- The briefing should've been done in a secluded environment devoid of distractions, such as the school's auditorium.

### What Unexpected Risk did we had to deal with?
- While we delt with the risk that came with dealing with pollution, we neglected the possible risk that could come with handling people during medical examinations. 

### How does our Project Management Process need to change to meet Future Goals
- We need to have more coordination and assess all of the possible risks with a deeper gaze to ensure that there are less unexpected issues and branching problems.
