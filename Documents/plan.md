# T.A.M.E
* Transfer - Shift Risk to Third Party
* Accept - Acknowledge the Risk and Deal with it if it Occurs
* Mitigate - Lessen the Risk by Reducing its Probabiliy and/or Impact
* Eliminate - Remove the Risk

Injury - When approaching the possibility of Injury, we found that it was highly likely to happen, and depending on what the volunteer was hurt with it could be extremely detrimental to the program as a whole. To combat this probability, we chose the Mitigate option of T.A.M.E. 

To Mitigate the possibility of risk, volunteers will be given proper safety equipment and will be given a briefing on how to clean up different types of pollution, what to do when you see someone get injured by pollution, and what to do when they get injured. (M)

Sickness - We realize that sickness from pollution is likely to happen, so when a case of sickness does arise we choose to use the Transfer option of T.A.M.E.

In terms of Transfer, we will allow the Protections for the Juvenile and Adolescent to handle the possible sicknesses that could arise with the help of their medical staff.
